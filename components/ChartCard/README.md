# ChartCard

A `card` that frames one chart: a `title` heading with a `small` subtitle, an inline legend on the right, the plot, and nothing else. Series take `chart-1` (current) and `chart-2` (comparison, the only hue) first, then `chart-3`…`chart-5`; because the scale differs by lightness, a greyscale print still reads. Gridlines are `border` at 1px, horizontal only; axes have no line, just `small` tick labels in `muted-foreground`, with numbers tabular and abbreviated (1.2M). Areas fill with the series colour at 12%. The tooltip is a `popover` with `shadow-sm` showing every series at the hovered x with the value in `mono`. Negative deltas are not red in charts; colour means series, and status colours stay out of plots. The consumer provides the data and series names; the system fixes the frame, scale, grid and legend.
