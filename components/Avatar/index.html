Guidelines

People and tenants in tables, headers and the sidebar: a `radius-full` circle (`radius-sm` square for tenants and systems) in 20, 24, 32 and 40px. With no image, initials in `body-medium` (11px at 20 and 24) on a `muted` ground in `foreground`; the system does not assign random colours to initials, because colour means status. An optional 8px presence dot in `success` sits bottom-right with a 2px `card` ring. Stacked avatars overlap by a quarter with the same ring, and show "+N" in `muted-foreground` after three. The consumer provides name, image and size.
